Test Focus: Detection should start immediately after navigating to a page. It must not require a zoom or scroll to begin.

1. On an iPad, open a page containing objects your enabled model recognizes, such as cars.
2. Follow a link or otherwise navigate within the page, then tap PLAY.
3. Confirm labels or detection boxes appear without touching, scrolling, or zooming the page.
4. Repeat after navigating to another page and after using Back or Forward.
5. Pause and restart detection. Confirm it resumes without requiring a page gesture.

Please report if PLAY produces no results until you zoom or scroll. Include the application log if possible.
