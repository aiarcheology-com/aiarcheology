Test Focus: New Settings toggle to disable detection animations (reduce CPU/GPU load).

What's new:

• Settings now has a "Disable Detection Animations" toggle. When on, detected and classified items show as static markers instead of the animated pulsing/fading circles and squares — reducing CPU/GPU use when many items are found at once.

Please test:

1. Open Settings and confirm the new "Disable Detection Animations" toggle appears in the detection/annotation group.
2. With it OFF (the default), run detection on a page and confirm the found-item circles/squares still pulse and fade as before.
3. Turn it ON, run detection again, and confirm found items still appear and clear but WITHOUT the pulse/fade animation (static markers), and that whole-image classification no longer flashes.
4. Toggle it back OFF and confirm the animation returns. The setting must persist across an app relaunch.
