Test Focus: A new "Remove Findings" reset in Settings — with the option to export your findings first — alongside the existing Restore Defaults.

What's new:

• Settings now has a "Reset" section with two independent, individually confirmed actions:
  – Remove Findings: permanently deletes ALL your saved findings and their photos. Before deleting, you're offered to export them first (exporting never deletes anything).
  – Restore Defaults: unchanged — it resets settings/models/history and recreates the demo, and still does NOT remove your findings.

Please test:

Remove Findings —
1. Save a few findings first (detect items on a page and store them) so you have findings to work with.
2. Open Settings and confirm a new "Reset" section appears containing "Remove Findings" and "Restore Defaults".
3. Tap "Remove Findings" and confirm a confirmation appears offering: Export First, Delete All Findings, and Cancel.
4. Tap "Cancel" and confirm nothing is deleted (your findings are still there).
5. Tap "Remove Findings" again and choose "Export First": confirm the share sheet appears with a findings export (a ZIP). Save or share it, then confirm that exporting did NOT delete anything — your findings are still present.
6. Tap "Remove Findings" once more and choose "Delete All Findings": confirm every finding AND its photo is removed (open the Findings list — it should be empty).
7. Repeat with zero findings saved: "Remove Findings" should not offer Export (nothing to export) and must not crash.

Restore Defaults (unchanged behavior) —
8. With some findings saved, run Settings → Restore Defaults and confirm your findings REMAIN (Restore Defaults resets settings/models/history but must not touch findings).
