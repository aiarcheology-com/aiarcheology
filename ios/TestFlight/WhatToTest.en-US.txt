Build: AIA-0757 (usability fixes from the last test session)

Test Focus: the fixes for what you reported last time — the sign-in row that stayed stuck offline,
the onboarding tour, washed-out detection colours, and several controls that were hard to find.

- Coming back online fixes itself. This is the important one. Turn on Airplane Mode and launch the
  app. Open the side menu: the account row at the bottom should say you are offline. Now open
  Settings and look for "Back Up My Findings" — while the app thinks it is offline, that whole
  setting is missing, which is what made backing up look broken. Turn the network back on and WAIT,
  without leaving the app and without backgrounding it. Within a few seconds the menu should show
  you signed in again, and the back-up setting should reappear in Settings on its own. Before, only
  quitting and reopening the app fixed it.

- The tour points at what to press. Settings, then Replay the introduction. Two things to watch.
  The Vladař site should already be loading behind the very first bubble — not the whole-country
  map, and not whatever page you happened to be on. And on every step that asks you to press
  something, that control should pulse so it is obvious which one it means. On the "switch a model
  on" step, the switch itself pulses. If you use Reduce Motion, the highlight is steady instead of
  pulsing — please check both if you can.

- Detection colours are vivid now. Import a model, or let a demo model detect something, and look
  at the boxes and points over the map. They should read clearly against the relief — no more
  washed-out or muddy colours. Please note: this applies to models added from now on. Models you
  already have keep the colour they were given, and you can change any of them by hand in that
  model's settings.

- The side menu reads properly. Open it. Findings, Settings and the sign-in row at the bottom
  should look like normal, tappable rows — not greyed out as if disabled. The old "+" in the top
  corner is now a filled blue button reading "Add Model", and the "Models" heading is gone.

- The sign-in screen says why. Tap the sign-in row at the bottom of the side menu. Under the
  welcome line it should now explain that signing in backs your findings up and shares them across
  your devices, rather than just repeating that you are signing in. Nothing there should suggest
  you need an account to detect anything — the app works signed out.

- Import is where you would expect it. Start a model download and open it. While it is downloading,
  the Import button sits in the middle of the form and is greyed out. When the download finishes it
  turns green and is the obvious thing to press. It is no longer up in the top corner, so there
  should be nothing tempting you to press Restart instead.

- Saving a finding shows the finding. Detect something and tap it to save. The Title box is now the
  first thing on the form, at the top. The picture should open zoomed in on the thing you picked,
  with some of the surroundings still visible so you can tell where it is — not the whole page. You
  can still pinch, drag, or use the slider to reframe it, and double-tap goes back to the full
  picture. Whatever you leave on screen is what gets saved, so please check the saved finding and
  its share picture look right.

- Back-up status is readable. Open Findings with something waiting to upload. The status line above
  the list is slightly larger than before, so it reads as status rather than fine print.

Please report anything that looks wrong, and say which device and iOS version you were on.
