Test Focus: After a good moment — saving a finding, or importing/converting a model — the app may now ask you to rate it on the App Store.

IMPORTANT: Apple does NOT show the rating dialog in TestFlight builds. You will not see it here, and that is expected — please do not report the missing dialog as a bug. What matters for this build is that the flows below still work exactly as before.

Please test:

1. Save a finding as usual (tap a detection, fill in the title, tap Save). Confirm the sheet closes normally, the finding appears in your inventory, and detection resumes as before.
2. Cancel a finding capture (swipe the sheet away, or leave without saving). Confirm nothing is saved and the app behaves exactly as before.
3. Import a CoreML model (Models → import). Confirm the success message appears and the model lands in your list.
4. Import a non-CoreML model that has to be converted. Confirm the conversion runs, the success message appears, and — when the app asks you to confirm the model's type — that the settings screen still opens right after the import sheet closes.
5. Open and close a model's settings from the list a few times. Confirm nothing unexpected appears.
6. Settings → ML Browser → Reset → "Restore Defaults": confirm it still works as before.
