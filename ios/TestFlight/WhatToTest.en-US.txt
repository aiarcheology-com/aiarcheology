Test Focus: Turning features off from our remote config now actually works. Previously a switch we flipped had no effect until the app was reinstalled — most importantly the one that controls whether the app asks you to subscribe at all.

Background: the app reads its remote settings at startup, but it was reading them a fraction too early — before they had arrived — so it always fell back to the values built into the app. This build makes each run remember what it received, and the next start uses it. A change we make therefore takes effect the second time you open the app, not the first.

Please test:

1. Normal use, first and foremost. Open the app, sign in, browse, run detections, save findings. Nothing should look or behave differently. This is the most important check in this build.
2. If you are a subscriber, confirm you still go straight into the app and are never shown the subscribe screen.
3. If you are not a subscriber, confirm you still see the subscribe screen exactly as before, and that Restore Purchases still works.
4. Force-quit the app and reopen it a few times. Nothing about subscribing, signing in, or your settings should change between launches.
5. Settings → Privacy: your analytics choice should be exactly as you left it, including after a force-quit.
6. Report anything where the app suddenly asks you to subscribe when it did not before, or lets you in when it should not have.
