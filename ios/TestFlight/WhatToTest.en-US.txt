Test Focus: Nothing should change. This build finishes reorganising the code that decides whether your subscription is active, so all of it can now be checked automatically. The behaviour is meant to be identical — the point of this round is to prove it is.

Background: the last piece was the step that reads a purchase from the App Store and turns it into "this person has paid". No test could reach it, which mattered most for refunds: if that step ever stopped noticing a refund, refunded users would keep their access and nothing would have caught it. It is now covered.

This build also contains changes to our own build and testing setup. They do not touch the app, so there is nothing extra to try because of them.

Please test:

1. If you are a subscriber, confirm you still go straight into the app and are never shown the subscribe screen. Force-quit and reopen a few times.
2. If you are not a subscriber, confirm you still see the subscribe screen exactly as before, and that Restore Purchases behaves the same.
3. If you are on the free trial, check the app still shows you as being on a trial everywhere it did before.
4. If you have ever been refunded for a subscription, confirm the app still treats you as not subscribed.
5. Subscribe, then use the app normally for a while. Access should not come and go between launches.
6. Report immediately if the app lets you in without a subscription, or asks you to subscribe when you already have one. Those are the two failures this change could plausibly cause, and they matter more than anything else in this build.
