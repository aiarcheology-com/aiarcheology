Build: Findings record the map page's own address again

Test Focus: Two changes. Saving a finding is now an aiming step with a crosshair, and a finding
taken on a map site that keeps its own address bar up to date records that address unchanged.

AIMING A FINDING
Tap Find (the crosshair button, top right). A large copy of that icon appears in the middle of the
web content. Tapping it always saves; its colour says what kind. RED means nothing was detected
under it and the place itself is stored. GREEN means a detected rectangle covers it and that
rectangle is stored.

- With no model running, tap Find and then the red crosshair. The finding form must open for the
  spot under it. This must work with detection switched off entirely.

- Only a small area in the very middle reacts to a tap. Everywhere else on the page — including
  most of the drawn crosshair — must still pan, pinch-zoom and flick normally. If the page will
  not move while the crosshair is up, that is the most important bug to report.

- Enable a model, start detection, then pan a detected rectangle under the crosshair. It must turn
  green; tapping it must open the form for that rectangle, with its image and label.

- Cancel in the hint bar must leave aiming mode at any point without saving anything.

- The guided tour is deliberately unchanged: its map lesson is still the old single tap, with no
  large crosshair. Run the tour from Settings and confirm it still completes.

THE PAGE ADDRESS SAVED WITH A FINDING
Reported on atlas.bayern.de: the address stored with a finding pointed at a different country.
Sites like that one write their own position into the address bar as you pan, and we were
overwriting it with our own reading of the map.

- On atlas.bayern.de, pan the map and watch the address bar change. Save a finding, then check the
  Page field in the finding form and in the saved finding: it must be exactly the address the
  browser is showing, character for character.

- Open that saved link afterwards. It must land on the same place you recorded, not somewhere else.

- KNOWN GAP, please test this specifically: the protection only engages once the app has been seen
  updating its own address. If you reopen the app straight onto a map page (Reopen Last Page) and
  save a finding WITHOUT panning first, the old behaviour can still apply and the address may be
  wrong. Please report whether that reproduces — it decides whether a further fix is needed.

- Sites that never change their address bar while you pan must still behave as before: the saved
  address should carry the view you were actually looking at, not the one you arrived with.
