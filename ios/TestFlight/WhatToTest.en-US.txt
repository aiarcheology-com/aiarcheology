Developer tooling only. This change adds a local "archive → TestFlight" delivery script
(scripts/deliver.sh) used to ship a build when Xcode Cloud is unavailable. There is NO user-facing
change: the app binary and behavior are identical to the previous build. This build is a plain
regression check that the core flows still work.

1. Launch & browse
- Open the app, sign in, and load a few pages. Nothing looks or behaves differently from the last build.

2. Detection & save (smoke test)
- Run a detection demo, capture a detection, and save a finding (title required). It saves and appears
  in the drawer → Findings, exactly as before.
- Persistence: force-quit and relaunch — the saved finding is still in Findings with its screenshot.
- Cancel path: start another capture, then dismiss the save sheet without saving — nothing is added
  to Findings and the app returns to browsing normally.
