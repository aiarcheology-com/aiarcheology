Test Focus: First ONNX object-detection model converts on your device

What's new:

• The on-device ONNX converter now handles its first object-detection model: Tiny YOLOv2 converts into a full Core ML detector — detection boxes and labels appear right in the browser, like the built-in demo detectors.

Please test:

1. Open the model importer and choose Download from URL.
2. Paste https://github.com/onnx/models/raw/main/validated/vision/object_detection_segmentation/tiny-yolov2/model/tinyyolov2-8.onnx and import (about 60 MB) — conversion should finish without errors.
3. Enable the converted model and browse to pages with photos of people, cars, animals, or furniture.
4. Confirm detection boxes with labels (person, car, dog, chair, …) appear over the photos.
5. Image classifiers should still convert and classify as before (MobileNetV2, ResNet-50, SqueezeNet).

Known limitations:

• Only raw-output YOLOv2-family detection models convert for now; detection models with embedded post-processing (e.g. SSD, tiny-yolov3) are still rejected with a clear message.
