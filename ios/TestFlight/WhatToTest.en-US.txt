Test Focus 1: Sharing a finding as a link, and what it carries.

Sharing still shares the picture; a link travels beside it and the card carries a QR. The link holds the FULL web address, including the part after the "?" that map sites keep the view in, so a recipient lands on YOUR view.

Test on a real map portal (ags.cuzk.cz, mapy.com, openstreetmap.org). Put coordinates into one finding first (Findings, tap one, GPS field, e.g. 48.8, 19.53) or the map part cannot be tested.

1. Share a finding from a map portal. Under the link switch is a box headed "This link carries at most:" showing the web address. Whatever is in that box is what travels. NEW: the copy button beside that heading puts exactly that address on the clipboard — paste it and compare.

2. Turn "Share the full web address" off: it shortens to the site alone, and copying again gives the shorter one. Turn it back on: the long form returns.

3. Send that link to yourself and open it on another device: it must land on the SAME map view. Shared with the switch off it lands on the site's front page instead — both are correct, the switch chooses.

4. The QR sits flush in the bottom-right CORNER of the information block, beside the rows not under them. Check Portrait and Landscape, Light and Dark, with a finding that has many rows and one with two; it must never overlap text.

5. Scan the QR with the Code Scanner (Control Centre), including from a photo of the card on another screen: it opens the app on the finding. The Camera app opens the web page instead — that is iOS, not a fault.

6. Turn "Let people open this finding in the app" off and share: "Save Image" returns and no link is attached.

7. Open a link to your OWN finding: the app opens read-only, saying it is yours. Nothing is added to your Findings list.

8. Open someone else's link. It appears as a CARD over the dimmed app, not full screen, with an orange "Shared by Someone Else" warning and a "Where on the page" diagram — no picture of your page leaves your device.

9. NEW, look hardest at this. On that card "Open the Original Page" is now the big button under the title, with the FULL address it opens printed beneath it, wrapping, plus a copy button. Read it before you tap: a link can no longer show a familiar site name while sending you elsewhere. The site name still appears in the details below.

10. Sign out or use a fresh install so the paywall, welcome screen or analytics question is still waiting, then open a shared link. The finding must appear ON TOP of it. Then tap "Open the Original Page": the card closes, you deal with the paywall, and land on the shared page.

11. Dismiss (top left) closes the card for good. Tapping outside it must NOT close it.

12. Damage a link (delete a chunk from the end) and open it: the app must say it could not read the link, never open to nothing.

13. Open a link on a device WITHOUT the app, or on a computer: a page describing the finding with a "Get ML Browser" button.

Test Focus 2: Findings carry the name of whoever found them.

The Save screen has an Author field between Note and GPS, prefilled from your account when signed in and empty otherwise. Save one with a name and one cleared — a blank author must show no row on the card, not "Unknown". Signed out it must never show your email. Export should include an author column.

Test Focus 3: Deleting a finding on a Mac.

On a Mac, right-click a finding row: Delete now sits beside Share, in red. It removes the finding and its picture just as swiping does on iPhone and iPad. Delete one, then the last one, and confirm the empty screen. Touch is unchanged: swipe left to delete, no menu on long press.

Most important: confirm every finding you had BEFORE this build is still there, with picture, title, note and GPS intact. If anything is missing, or the app will not start, tell us at once and say which version you came from.

Please report your device model, iOS version and the language you tested in.
