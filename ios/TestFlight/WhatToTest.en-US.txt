Build: Detection and pages that reload themselves

Test Focus: On heavy map sites the page could reload itself every few minutes while detection was
running. The app now steps out of the way when that happens instead of walking straight back into
it. Nothing about what is captured or stored has changed in this build.

- Reproduce on a heavy GIS site. The reported one is the Hessen wind atlas
  (umweltdaten.hessen.de); zbgis.skgeodesy.sk and ags.cuzk.cz are the same family. Start detection
  with Find and leave it running for 15-20 minutes. Previously the page reloaded roughly every 2-7
  minutes and detection carried straight on. Now the FIRST reload should pause detection briefly
  and resume it once the page has finished loading; if the same site does it AGAIN within ten
  minutes, detection should stop and stay stopped until you press the button again.

- Please report how often you end up restarting detection, and whether the page still reloads with
  detection stopped. Some reloading is expected to survive this: measured, the page on its own
  accounts for about 970 MB of the roughly 1,190 MB the web content was using, so it can exhaust
  memory without any help from us. This is meant to cut how often it happens, not to guarantee it
  never does.

- Check the ordinary paths did not regress: detection keeps running normally across page loads on
  a light map site (mapy.cz); following a link while detection runs does not stop it; saving a
  finding and closing the sheet still resumes detection afterwards.

- iPad 8th generation (3 GB memory) is the device this was reported on and the likeliest to show
  it. A newer iPad may not reproduce it at all.

STILL WORTH RETESTING FROM THE PREVIOUS BUILD

- The page address saved with a finding: under the Page field there is a note saying the address
  may be fixed and may not carry the position you captured. Please say whether it reads clearly,
  and whether it still looks right at the largest text sizes (Settings > Accessibility > Display &
  Text Size > Larger Text, with "Larger Accessibility Sizes" on). On geoland.at the saved address
  carries no position at all, so reopening it shows the whole of Austria while the GPS value is
  correct — that is the situation the note is there to explain.

- Aiming a finding: tap Find, then the large crosshair in the middle of the web content. RED stores
  the place under it, GREEN stores a detected rectangle covering it. Only a small area in the very
  middle takes the tap; everywhere else must still pan and pinch-zoom normally.

- KNOWN GAP, please test specifically: on a map site that writes its own position into the address
  bar (atlas.bayern.de), the protection against us overwriting that address only engages once the
  app has seen the site update it. If you reopen the app straight onto such a page (Reopen Last
  Page) and save a finding WITHOUT panning first, the address may still be wrong. Please report
  whether that reproduces — it decides whether a further fix is needed.
