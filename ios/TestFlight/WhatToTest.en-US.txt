Test Focus 1: Findings can now carry the name of whoever found them, and that name appears on the share card.

A finding has a new optional "Author" field. If you are signed in, it is filled in for you when you save a finding; if you are not signed in, it starts empty. Either way you can type whatever you like, or leave it blank. Things with your name on them are worth more than anonymous ones — that is the whole point of this change.

1. Sign in, then find something and save it. The Save screen has a new Author field between Note and GPS, already filled in with your account name. Save, then open the finding from the Findings list and share it. Your name should appear on the card, just under the detection label. Check both Portrait and Landscape, and both Light and Dark.

2. Save another finding, but this time clear the Author field before saving. The card must simply not show an author row at all — no "Unknown", no empty line, no gap where a name would be.

3. Start saving a finding, clear the Author field, then switch to another app and come back without closing the Save screen. The field must STAY empty — your name must not reappear over a value you deleted. (Closing the Save screen and starting a brand new capture is different: that is a new finding, so the name is filled in again on purpose.)

4. Sign out, then save a finding. The Author field should be empty, and never show your email address — an email is not a name and must never end up on a picture you post. Type a name by hand and confirm it saves and shows on the card.

5. Open an OLD finding you saved before this build, from the Findings list. It should open normally with an empty Author field. Type a name, tap Save, then go back into it — the name should still be there. Then check a DIFFERENT old finding: it must still have no author. Editing one finding's author must never touch another.

6. Sign out and back in while you have findings with authors saved. Their authors must be exactly as you left them. Signing out must never rewrite or erase a name already saved.

7. Export your findings (Findings list → Export) and open the ZIP. Both findings.json and findings.csv should include an author column, with your name where you set one and empty where you did not.

Most important: this build also changes how findings are stored. Please confirm that every finding you had BEFORE installing it is still there, with its picture, title, note and GPS intact. If anything is missing, or the app will not start, tell us immediately and say which version you updated from.

Please report your device model, iOS version and the language you tested in.
