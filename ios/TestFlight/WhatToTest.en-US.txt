Test Focus: configurable annotation sounds.

- Open a detection model’s configuration, then Annotation > Sound. With the setting off, the
  Annotation row must say Disabled. Turn it on, select and preview several of the eleven sounds
  (including Sonar), adjust the volume, then close and reopen the configuration to confirm the
  choices persist for that model.

- Choose different sounds for two detection models. Run them over a page with several detected
  objects: each newly painted rectangle, point, or circle should play the sound selected for its
  model. When several annotations arrive together, two sounds can play at once and the remaining
  sounds should follow promptly; neither model should suppress the other.

- Leave detection running as results arrive progressively. Existing annotations must not play a
  second time when a later result repeats them, while a genuinely new annotation should play once.

- Confirm the Sound preview and automatic annotation sounds do not change the device’s system
  volume. If practical, also test with other audio already playing and with the silent switch on.

Please report your device model, iOS version, model(s), selected sound and volume, and a short
description of the annotation sequence if anything sounds incorrect.
