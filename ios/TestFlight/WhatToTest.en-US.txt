Test Focus: Subscription Plan now shows the billing term + a "test purchase" badge on Subscribe
Build: TBD

What's new in this build — two subscription-screen changes:
1. The Subscription screen's "Plan" row used to show only a price (e.g. "$3.99") with no hint
   of whether that was monthly or yearly. It now shows the billing term too, e.g.
   "$3.99 / month" or "$39.99 / year".
2. The paywall "Subscribe" button now carries a loud yellow wrench-and-screwdriver badge with a
   blue border. This marks it as a TEST purchase: in TestFlight, payments go through Apple's
   Sandbox and you are NOT charged real money. The badge is there so you know it's safe to tap.
   (It is hidden in the real App Store release.)

Reminder: you need an account and (with payments on) an active subscription or free trial to get
past the paywall. TestFlight purchases use Apple's Sandbox — no real charge; trial/renewal periods
are accelerated.

Devices / Platforms
- iPhone (required)
- iPad (if available)

1. Plan billing term (primary focus)
- Subscribe (or be in your trial), then open Settings → Account → Subscription.
- Look at the "Plan" row. It should show the price AND the period, e.g. "$3.99 / month"
  (localized). It must be clear whether the plan is monthly or yearly — not just a bare price.
- Status and Renews / Free-trial-until rows should look unchanged.

2. "Test purchase" badge on Subscribe (primary focus)
- Open the paywall (the Subscribe screen behind the gate, or Settings → Upgrade).
- The green "Subscribe" button should have a blue border and a yellow badge with a
  wrench/screwdriver icon in its top-right corner.
- The badge is decorative — the button must still tap and start the (sandbox) purchase exactly
  as before. Confirm tapping Subscribe still works.
- The free-trial line under the price (when you're still trial-eligible) should read the same as
  before, e.g. "Free for 1 month, then $3.99".

3. Light regression
- Restore Purchases and Manage Subscription still open Apple's native sheets.
- Browse a couple of normal websites and run object detection to confirm nothing else changed.

4. Report Issues

  * Device model and iOS version
  * Screenshot or screen recording if possible
