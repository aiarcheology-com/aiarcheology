Test Focus: choose hardware acceleration for one model.

Open a model’s configuration, then open Hardware Acceleration. Automatic is the normal setting and
should be selected by default. Choose each available option one at a time, close and reopen the
configuration, and confirm the choice is kept for that model only.

- On an iPhone, Neural Engine, Graphics Processor, and Processor Only should be available. On a
  device where an accelerator is unavailable, its option remains visible but is disabled and says
  it is not available on that device.
- Run a detector after selecting each available option. Detections should remain plausible;
  Processor Only can take much longer. Automatic, Neural Engine, and Graphics Processor make no
  promised speed ordering.
- If you have two configured models, change one and confirm the other still has its own setting.

Please report your device model, iOS version, selected hardware option, and language if anything
looks wrong.
