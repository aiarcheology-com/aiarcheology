Test Focus 1: The model-import sheet, in your own language.

Every language except English was telling users the wrong thing about which models the app accepts. It named the wrong Apple product ("Create ML" instead of "Core ML"), left out ONNX import entirely, and left out image classification. English was always correct.

This only needs checking if you use the app in a language other than English.

1. Switch your device to German, Czech, French, Spanish, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian, Hungarian, Arabic, Hebrew, Hindi, Persian, Bengali or any Chinese variant.

2. Open the model list, tap the + button to import a model, and read the "Supported Models" paragraph.

3. It must say Core ML — not Create ML — and it must mention that ONNX (.onnx) models can be imported and are converted on your device. It should also mention image classification alongside object detection.

4. The terms Core ML, ONNX, .mlmodel, .mlpackage and .onnx must appear exactly like that, untranslated.

5. Sanity check the rest of that screen in your language: nothing else changed, so anything that reads oddly is pre-existing — tell us anyway.

Please report your device model, iOS version and the language you tested in.
