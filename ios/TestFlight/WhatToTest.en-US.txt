Test Focus: The analytics-consent question now appears AFTER sign-in and the paywall — not before — and pre-consent paywall activity is handled correctly.

What's new:

• The analytics opt-in ("Enable" / "Not now") no longer greets you on launch. You sign in and pass the paywall first; the analytics question appears only afterward, just before the main app.
• If you opt in, usage from the paywall step that happened before you answered is still counted. If you choose "Not now," none of it is recorded or sent — nothing leaves the device without consent.

Please test:

1. Fresh install (or Settings → Restore Defaults, then relaunch): confirm the SIGN-IN screen appears first — NOT the analytics question.
2. Sign in, then get past the paywall (subscribe or free trial). Confirm the analytics question appears only now, right before the browser.
3. Choose "Not now": confirm you enter the app and Settings shows analytics OFF.
4. On another run, choose "Enable": confirm the app works normally and Settings shows analytics ON.
5. Confirm the analytics question never appears before you are signed in and past the paywall.

Note: seeing the paywall more than once may count more than once after you opt in — that's expected. If the analytics feature is turned off server-side, no analytics question appears at all (unchanged).
