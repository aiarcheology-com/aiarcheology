Test Focus 1: The application log attached to a support report. Until now it recorded almost nothing in TestFlight and App Store builds, and it was thrown away every time the app closed — so a report almost never described the problem it was about.

1. Settings > ML Browser > About the app has a new "Debug" section with a switch, "Keep a detailed log", already ON. Read the sentence under it and tell us if it is unclear.

2. Use the app for a few minutes — browse, run a detection, download a model. Then Settings > About > Report a problem, turn on "Include application log", and tap Compose. Open the attached file: it should hold a lot of detail, not a handful of lines. Nothing should freeze while it is prepared.

3. The important one: make the app record something, then FORCE-QUIT it (swipe it away), reopen it, and compose a report. The log must still contain what happened before you quit. Previously it started from scratch every launch.

4. Turn the switch OFF (About the app > Debug) and compose again — the attachment is much shorter, and the app must not feel any different to use. Clearing your browsing history also deletes the stored log, on purpose: it holds the addresses you visited. "Restore Defaults" does not delete it.

5. With "Include application log" on, use the new "Save or share the log" row to send it to Files, AirDrop or a messaging app. The filename says "unredacted" only when you turned on "Send unredacted data". This is mainly for people whose device has no Mail account — that route could not attach anything before.

6. Fail a model download on purpose: turn on Airplane Mode partway through a multi-file download. The log should name the file that failed and give a reason.

Test Focus 2: Two small UI corrections.

1. Open the left drawer. The account row at the bottom (Sign In, or your email when signed in) should now look like the Findings and Settings rows directly above it — same corner rounding, same height.

2. Open a model's settings and look at the Symbol picker. The middle option is now called "Rectangle" instead of "Square" — it always drew a rectangle, only the label was wrong. If you had "Square" selected before this build, it must still be selected (now reading "Rectangle") and detections must look exactly as before. If it switched itself to Circle, report that immediately.

Test Focus 3: Downloading models from the catalog on a real iPhone or iPad. Multi-file downloads used to fail on physical devices only.

1. Download a multi-file Core ML package, for example "yolo11l-int8" from TheCluster/YOLOv11-CoreML. It should install without an error, appear in your model list, and run one detection.

2. Repeat with an ONNX model and with a plain single-file .mlmodel — these take different internal paths.

3. Download the same model twice and cancel one partway, then check the app's storage size in Settings; repeated downloads should not leave it growing.

Test Focus 4: Subscribing while you still have free days. This is the one we most need eyes on — it cannot be covered by our automated tests, because those cannot show a real App Store product.

You need a device whose free access is STILL RUNNING (Settings > ML Browser shows "Free until <date>" under the Subscription row). Deleting and reinstalling does NOT give you a fresh 14 days on purpose, so if yours has already run out, this focus is not testable for you — say so and skip it.

1. Settings > ML Browser > Subscription > tap the green Subscribe button. A confirmation must appear FIRST, saying how many free days you still have and that they are not added to your subscription. If the button instead does nothing at all, report that immediately — that is the failure we are most worried about.

2. Take "Keep My Free Days" first. It should simply close and charge nothing. Do this before step 3: subscribing uses up your free trial and you cannot get back to this state.

3. Read the sentence under the Subscription row in Settings. It should no longer suggest that subscribing gives you an "extra" trial on top of the days you have left — it should say the trial starts from the moment you subscribe. Tell us if it still reads as though the two add up.

4. Only if you are willing to spend the trial: tap Subscribe again and choose "Subscribe Anyway". It should behave exactly as subscribing always has.

5. If your Apple Account has already used this trial before, you are charged immediately rather than getting a trial. Check that the confirmation still appears and that nothing on the screen promises you a free trial.

6. The Subscription row's label used to be grey, as though the row were disabled. It should now be the same black as every other row in Settings, and tapping anywhere along the row — not just the word — must still open the subscribe screen.

7. Unchanged, but please still exercise: the Dismiss button on the subscribe screen, Restore Purchases, and Manage Subscription.

Please report your device model and iOS version with any problem, and attach the log if you can.
