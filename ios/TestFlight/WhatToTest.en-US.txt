Build: AIA-0757, AIA-0761, AIA-0748, AIA-0758, AIA-0735, AIA-0779, AIA-0782

Test Focus: your existing findings must survive the update, a shared link must point at the right
place, an edit on one device must reach the other on its own, and the subscription screen must
never be a dead end.

- YOUR EXISTING FINDINGS MUST ALL STILL BE THERE. Storage changed in this build. Update from the
  build you were running rather than deleting the app first — a fresh install cannot test this.
  Open Findings: every finding still listed, with its picture, title and red box in the right place.

- TWO DEVICES, ONE ACCOUNT. Sign both into the same account. On A, open Findings and leave it open.
  On B, edit a finding's title. On A, close Findings and open it again: B's change should be there
  without you pressing anything.

- Pull down on the findings list to fetch again. The spinner must stay until the fetch has really
  finished.

- Synchronize (the renamed button) still works both ways: in Airplane Mode it should say there is
  no connection, on a working connection that everything is up to date. Its question mark explains
  what it does — please tell us whether that answers the question you would have asked.

- IF YOU HAVE EVER TAPPED "Keep on This Device": Synchronize must leave those findings local,
  however many times you press it. Only "Back Up My Findings", now inside the question-mark sheet,
  still sends them. Check both halves.

- EVERY ROW SAYS WHETHER IT IS IN THE CLOUD. Green cloud with a tick = backed up and current; grey
  upload cloud = not up there yet; grey cloud with a line through it = kept on this device. Signed
  OUT there must be no icon at all, and signing in as a different account must recalculate the
  marks rather than still showing the previous account's.

- A shared link points where the detection actually was. Before saving, pinch and drag to crop in
  tight — the case that was broken. Share it with the link on and open it: under "Where on the
  page" the mark should sit where the thing was. Cropping harder must not move it. Findings saved
  before this build show no "Where on the page" section at all: deliberate.

- Findings toolbar: Export and Import now sit inside one button called Data, beside an arrows
  button for sorting. Check the title is not squashed, also with Larger Text turned up.

- Sorting: Date (the default and the old order), Alphabetical, Website, plus Reverse. Worth real
  attention: a finding with no website sits at the bottom under Website and must STILL be there
  after Reverse. Same with no date or no title. The choice resets to Date when you reopen Findings.

- Coming back online fixes itself. Airplane Mode on, launch the app, then turn the network on and
  WAIT in the app: you should be signed in again and "Back Up My Findings" should reappear on its
  own.

- The tour points at what to press: Settings, then Replay the introduction. With Reduce Motion the
  highlight is steady instead. Detection colours are vivid now; models you have keep theirs.

- NEW: deleting your account offers your findings first. On the subscription screen tap Delete
  Account. If this device has saved findings, the confirmation now says they are NOT deleted with
  the account and offers "Export First…" — check the ZIP really reaches the share sheet, and that
  the warning that deleting does not cancel App Store billing is still there. With nothing saved,
  that export choice must NOT appear.

- NEW: subscribing on an Apple Account that is already subscribed. Needs a Sandbox or TestFlight
  account that already holds the subscription. Tap Subscribe; if Apple answers that you are
  currently subscribed, tap Cancel. Before, that left you stuck with nothing to do. Now you should
  be let straight in, or get an alert titled "Subscription Found" telling you to tap Restore
  Purchases, which should then work. On an account that is NOT subscribed, cancelling a real
  purchase sheet must still say nothing at all.
