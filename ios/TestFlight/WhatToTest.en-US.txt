Build: 1.0.11 — Location search cancellation fix

This beta contains a small location-search fix. Cancelled searches now stop before publishing an old filter. It does not establish that all reported app hangs are fixed; please continue reporting freezes during launch or normal use.

LOCATION SEARCH

- Open the address sheet and type quickly into the location search field. After you pause, the history list should match your final query, without jumping back to an earlier query.
- Clear the field: the full history should return. Type another query, dismiss the sheet while filtering, and reopen it. The history should be restored.
- With a web page already open, open the address sheet. Its current address should remain prefilled without hiding other history. Filter the list, dismiss the sheet, and reopen it: the address and unfiltered history should return.
- Repeat the steps several times, including after sending the app to the background and reopening it. Check on iPhone, iPad and the Mac if available.

GENERAL RESPONSIVENESS

- Cold-launch the app several times and use browsing, tabs and findings normally. Report any freeze, whether during launch, typing, changing pages or returning from the background.
- If a freeze occurs, note the device and OS version, the approximate time, the screen and action just before it, and whether the app recovered or had to be force-quit. Never include private page content or credentials in a report.

REGRESSION CHECKS

- Sign in, sign out and sign in again. The sign-in sheet should close on its own and Settings should show the correct account.
- Save and reopen a finding. On a map page, check that its saved image, Page and GPS refer to the same view.
- A fresh installation should show the neutral Welcome, the tour and then the analytics question, with no payment or trial requirement. Returning users should not see Welcome again.
