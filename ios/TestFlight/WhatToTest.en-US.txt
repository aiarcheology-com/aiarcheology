Test Focus: Browse and install models from the Open Catalog (AIA-0413)

Build: 1.0.36 (123) — July 13, 2026

What's new:

• Open Catalog adds a third way to acquire models, alongside Files and URL.
• Search Hugging Face for Core ML image-classification and object-detection models.
• Single-file .mlmodel downloads reuse the existing URL download and import experience.
• .mlpackage models download their package contents, then use the normal import and validation flow.

Please test:

1. Open the model importer and choose Open Catalog.
2. Search for a model such as "mobilenet" or "yolo". Confirm results appear after a brief pause and show loading, empty, or error states appropriately.
3. Select a result with an .mlmodel file. Confirm the familiar download detail screen opens and that a successful download imports the model.
4. If a result offers an .mlpackage, download it and keep the app open until import finishes. Confirm cancellation works while the package is downloading.
5. Search again after a failed or empty query, then close and reopen the catalog. The importer should remain responsive.

Known limitations:

• Only public Hugging Face Core ML image-classification and object-detection models are listed.
• Models whose type cannot be determined may remain Disabled until you assign their type in the model configuration.
