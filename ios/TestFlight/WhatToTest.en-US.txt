Test Focus 1 (NEW, look hardest here): what the app says about a PyTorch model file.

This build changed how model checkpoints are read. Nothing about it is visible on screen except the
message you get when you import one - so that message IS the test.

1. Download from URL (Model Import > Download from URL) using:
   https://zenodo.org/records/15848663/files/OD_ringfort.tar?download=1
   It is ~518 MB, so use Wi-Fi. When it finishes the app must refuse it, saying it "describes
   itself as an ADAF Ringfort detection model" and that converting it is not supported yet. A
   cryptic failure, a hang, or a message naming no model is the bug. Refusing is correct.

2. Try OD_barrow.tar and OD_AO.tar from the same record: they must say "Barrow" and
   "All archaeology". A WRONG name here matters more than any other result in this build.

3. Rename OD_ringfort.tar to anything else before importing. It must still say "Ringfort" - the name
   comes from inside the file, not from the filename.

4. Try one HRNet file from that record (the long ..._with_Transformation.tar): it must say
   "image segmentation model" and that those are not supported - a DIFFERENT message from item 1.

5. Feed it something that is not a model - a photo renamed to .tar. It must fail the ordinary
   import failure (NOT the messages above), promptly, with no hang and no cleanup left behind.

6. Watch memory and battery during 1-4: reading these files should be quick and must NOT feel like
   the app is loading half a gigabyte. If the app is killed while importing, tell us at once.

7. Model import you already rely on must be UNCHANGED: .mlmodel and .onnx by URL, Open Catalog, and
   Files import. A model that imported before and is refused now is the most serious thing you can
   find. The download hint still reads ".mlmodel or .onnx" - check it in each language you read.

Test Focus 2: sharing a finding as a link.

Put coordinates in one finding first (Findings, tap one, GPS, e.g. 48.8, 19.53) and use a real map
portal (ags.cuzk.cz, mapy.com, openstreetmap.org), or the map part cannot be tested.

- Share a finding. The box "This link carries at most:" shows exactly what travels, with a copy
  button - paste and compare.
- Turn "Share the full web address" off: it shortens to the site alone. On again: the long form
  returns.
- Open that link on another device: same map view with the switch on, the site's front page with it
  off. Both are correct; the switch chooses.
- The QR sits flush in the bottom-right corner of the information block. Portrait and Landscape,
  Light and Dark, a finding of many rows and one of two - it must not overlap text.
- Scan the QR with the Code Scanner (Control Centre): it opens the app on the finding. The Camera
  app opens the web page - that is iOS, not a bug.
- Open someone else's link: a CARD over the dimmed app with an orange "Shared by Someone Else"
  warning, "Open the Original Page" as the big button, and the FULL address printed beneath it.
  Read it before you tap. Dismiss (top left) closes it; tapping outside must NOT.
- Damage a link (delete a chunk from the end): the app must say it could not read it, never open to
  nothing.

Test Focus 3: findings carry a name, and delete on a Mac.

The Save screen has an Author field between Note and GPS, prefilled from your account when signed in
and empty otherwise. A blank author must show no row on the card, not "Unknown"; signed out it must
never show your email. On a Mac, right-click a finding row: Delete sits beside Share, in red, and
removes the finding and its picture just as swiping does on iPhone.

Most important: confirm every finding you had BEFORE this build is still there, with picture, title,
note and GPS intact. If anything is missing, or the app will not start, tell us at once and say
which version you came from.

Please report your device model, iOS version and the language you tested in.
