Build: AIA-713 (findings back-up)

Test Focus: backing your findings up to your account, bringing them back from an exported archive,
and confirming that findings you already had are never quietly changed. Screenshots are also stored
differently in this build.

Please read this first: once you sign in, this build can upload your findings — the title, your
note, the finder credit, any coordinates you typed, and the screenshot — to your account, so they
survive a lost or replaced phone. Nothing is uploaded while you are signed out. If you sign in on a
device that already has findings, the app asks before uploading them; findings you make afterwards
while signed in are uploaded as you make them. You can stop it at any time in Settings.

- Signing in offers to back up what is already there. With a few findings on the device, sign in.
  A card should ask whether to back up the findings already on this device, and it should name the
  right number. Tap Keep on This Device. Nothing should upload. Make a new finding: that one SHOULD
  upload, because you made it while signed in.

- The button, and changing your mind. Open Findings; the green Back Up My Findings button is pinned
  at the bottom and should stay visible however long the list is. Tap it. It should confirm the
  back-up, and it should also lift the earlier Keep on This Device decision — that button is the
  explicit yes.

- Restoring on a clean device. Delete the app, reinstall it, and sign in with the same account.
  Your findings should come back, pictures included, without importing anything.

- Turning it off really stops it. In Settings, switch backing up off. Make a new finding: it must
  not upload. Now delete a finding that WAS backed up — that delete must still reach the account,
  so it does not reappear on another device. Switch back on and confirm uploads resume.

- Signing out leaves your findings alone. Sign out. Every finding must still be on the device.
  Sign in as a different account on the same phone: that account must not see the first one's
  findings, and the card should ask before anything of theirs is uploaded.

- Offline is not an error. Turn on Airplane Mode, make two findings, then tap Back Up My Findings.
  It should say you are offline rather than claiming success. Turn the network back on: they should
  upload on their own, without you tapping anything.

- Export, then bring it back. Open the drawer > Findings and tap Export; save or AirDrop the ZIP
  somewhere you can reach it. Delete a couple of findings (swipe), then tap Import and pick that
  ZIP. The deleted findings should return with their titles, notes, pages, and their pictures
  intact. Findings you did not delete should be untouched.

- Importing the same file twice must not duplicate anything. Import the same ZIP again. You should
  be asked what to do about findings that are already on the device: choose Keep Mine. The summary
  should say nothing was restored and everything was skipped, and your list should look exactly as
  it did before.

- Replace does what it says. Import the same ZIP once more and choose Replace this time. The count
  of findings must not grow — the archived copies overwrite the ones already there rather than
  landing beside them.

- A file that is not a findings export should be refused politely. Tap Import and pick any other
  ZIP (a downloaded model archive, for instance). Expect a plain message that it is not a findings
  archive, and no change whatsoever to your findings list. The app must not hang or quit.

- An archive from the previous release still imports. If you have a ZIP exported by an older build
  of the app, import it. It should restore normally — older exports are expected to keep working.

- New screenshots are smaller. Capture a few new findings. They should look the same in the list
  and on the share card as before. Please flag it if a picture looks noticeably blocky or soft
  compared with findings captured in an earlier build, especially when a detection box is small.

- Findings saved before this build must still open. Anyone upgrading with existing findings should
  see them all, with their pictures, exactly as before. A missing picture or an empty list here is
  the most important thing to report.
