Build: AIA-0757, AIA-0761, AIA-0748 (usability fixes, a corrected share link, findings sorting)

Test Focus: three builds' worth of changes reached you at once. The two that matter most are that
your existing findings survive the update, and that a shared link points at the right thing.

- YOUR EXISTING FINDINGS MUST ALL STILL BE THERE. This build changes how findings are stored, so
  please check first: open Findings and confirm every finding you had is still listed, with its
  picture, title and red box in the right place. Update from the build you were running rather
  than deleting the app first — a fresh install cannot test this at all.

- Coming back online fixes itself. Airplane Mode on, launch the app. The account row in the side
  menu says offline, and "Back Up My Findings" is missing from Settings — that absence is what made
  backing up look broken. Turn the network on and WAIT, without leaving or backgrounding the app.
  Within seconds you should be signed in again and the setting should reappear on its own.

- A shared link points where the detection actually was. Save a finding and, before saving, pinch
  and drag to crop in tight — that is the case that was broken. Share it with the link switched on
  and open it. Under "Where on the page" the mark should sit where the thing was on the page as you
  saw it in the browser. Cropping harder must not move it: save the same detection twice, once
  uncropped and once cropped hard, and both links should mark the same place at the same size.
  Findings saved before this build show no "Where on the page" section at all — deliberate, not a
  bug: their original position was thrown away and there is nothing truthful left to send.

- The Findings toolbar changed shape. Export and Import are now inside one button called Data, next
  to a new arrows button for sorting. Check the title is not squashed, and check again with Larger
  Text turned up high. Export and import themselves work exactly as before from their new home.

- Sorting by date, name and website. Tap the arrows: Date, Alphabetical, Website, plus Reverse.
  Date is the default and is the order the list has always used. The part worth real attention: a
  finding with no website sits at the bottom under Website — and after tapping Reverse it must
  STILL be at the bottom, not jump to the top. Same for one with no date or no title. A row with no
  information should never lead the list. The choice resets to Date when you reopen Findings.

- The tour points at what to press. Settings, then Replay the introduction. The Vladař site should
  already be loading behind the first bubble, and each step's control should pulse. With Reduce
  Motion the highlight is steady instead — please check both if you can.

- Detection colours are vivid now. New models get clear colours against the relief; models you
  already have keep theirs, and you can change any by hand in that model's settings.

- Smaller things. The side menu rows read as tappable, with a blue "Add Model" button. Import sits
  in the middle of the download form and turns green when ready. The save form opens with Title
  first and the picture zoomed on what you picked. Back-up status above the Findings list is
  larger.

Please report anything that looks wrong, and say which device and iOS version you were on.
