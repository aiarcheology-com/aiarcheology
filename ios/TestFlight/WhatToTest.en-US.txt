Build: Findings sheets

Test Focus: Findings sheets should have room to show their content on iPad and on a Mac running the
iPad app, without sacrificing controls in a compact window.

- On an iPad or Mac, save a finding and open the findings list. The sheets should be comfortably
  larger than the old form sheet, while Cancel, Save, and navigation controls remain visible in
  Split View or a smaller window.

- Share a finding with a long coordinate. The card preview must remain visible, and the controls
  below it must scroll rather than truncating their explanatory text. Rotate an iPhone to landscape
  and confirm the preview still remains visible.

- Tap the expand button at the preview's upper-left. The full card should open at reading size;
  check the coordinate is complete, then tap Done to return to the composer.
