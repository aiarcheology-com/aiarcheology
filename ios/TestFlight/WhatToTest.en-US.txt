Test Focus: Updated Privacy Policy (+ Sign-In / Subscription regression)
Build: TBD

What's new in this build — the in-app Privacy Policy has been fully replaced with an accurate,
self-contained version (no third-party trackers; covers all regions where ML Browser is offered).
Please confirm it opens and reads correctly. Sign-in and subscription are unchanged from the last
build — a short regression pass on those follows.

Privacy Policy (new in this build)
- Open the Privacy Policy from BOTH places it is linked:
  * the paywall (before subscribing): tap the "Privacy Policy" link
  * Settings / About: open the Privacy Policy entry
- Confirm it loads instantly (it is bundled, so it works offline), renders cleanly with no broken
  layout or missing text, and is readable in both Light and Dark mode.
- Tap the contact email link and confirm it opens a pre-addressed mail draft.
- Scroll the whole document — region sections (EU/UK, US, Canada, Brazil, Australia, children)
  should all be present, and there should be no loading spinners or external content.

Reminder for the rest of the build: you need an account to use the app, and (with payments on)
an active subscription or free trial to get past the paywall. TestFlight purchases use Apple's
Sandbox — you are NOT charged real money, and trial/renewal periods are accelerated.

Devices / Platforms
- iPhone (required)
- iPad (if available)

1. Sign In
- On launch you should see a sign-in screen.
- Sign in any of these ways and confirm it proceeds:
  * Email and password (create an account or sign in)
  * Continue with Apple
  * Continue with Google
  * Continue with Facebook — testers only for now; if it's not enabled for your account,
    use email/Apple/Google instead

2. Subscription Paywall (Primary Focus)
- Right after signing in (if you don't already have a subscription) the whole app is gated by
  a paywall titled "ML Browser / Subscribe to unlock the full app."
  * It should show the app logo, the plan, "$3.99", and "Free for 1 month, then $3.99".
  * (On a returning account that already used the trial, you'll see just "$3.99/month" — no
    free-trial line. That's correct.)
- Tap Subscribe → confirm the Sandbox purchase → the paywall should disappear and the app opens.
  * No real charge. The free month starts now.
- From the paywall (before subscribing) also confirm: Restore Purchases, Sign Out (→ back to
  sign-in), Delete Account, and the Terms of Use / Privacy Policy links all work.
- On iPad: the paywall content/buttons should be centered, not stretched edge-to-edge.

3. Subscription Management (once subscribed / in trial)
- Open the drawer → Settings → Account → tap "Subscription".
  * The submenu should show Plan ($3.99), Status (Free trial or Active), and a Renews date.
  * "Manage Subscription" should open Apple's subscription sheet (where you can cancel/change).
  * "Restore Purchases" and "Request a Refund" should work (these use Apple's real sheets).
- Cancel the trial in the Apple sheet, then relaunch the app once the period lapses — you should
  be returned to the paywall (access correctly removed).

4. Account Management
- Drawer → Settings → Manage Account → try Change Password.
- Sign Out → returns to the sign-in screen; you can't use the app until you sign in again.
- Delete Account (use a throwaway account — permanently deletes it) → returns to sign-in.
  Note: deleting your account does NOT cancel an Apple subscription — cancel that separately.

5. Offline Behavior
- Turn off Wi-Fi/cellular and launch (or foreground) the app.
  * You should see a "No connection" screen with a Try Again button.
  * Reconnect, tap Try Again, and you should get back in.

6. Core Regression (once past the paywall)
- Browse websites and confirm pages load normally.
- Run object detection and verify annotations appear correctly.
- Import a model from Files and verify it works.

Beta note: the sign-in screen has a Development/Production switch so we can test either
environment. Leave it on Development unless asked otherwise.

7. Report Issues
Please report any issues with:
  * What you were doing (steps to reproduce)
  * Device model and iOS version
  * Screenshot or screen recording if possible
