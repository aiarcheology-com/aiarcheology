Build: AIA-0757, AIA-0761, AIA-0748, AIA-0758, AIA-0735, AIA-0779, AIA-0782, AIA-0763, AIA-0765, AIA-0783, AIA-0785, AIA-0778, AIA-0791

Test Focus: detection boxes must sit on what they found, the two questions the app asks must never
share a screen, and your existing findings must survive the update.

- NEW: THE BOX MUST BE ON THE OBJECT. Turn on a detection model, set the shape to Rectangle and
  turn labels on. Point it at a page with several objects. The blue box must sit ON the thing, not
  below it — labels used to push it down the page, further the more classes were found. Compare a
  page whose picture already has boxes drawn into it: ours should line up with those.

- NEW: ONE LABEL, AND YOU CAN READ IT. Each box gets a single label, "class 0.42", above it — not
  a row of six-letter fragments. It must be readable on a busy photo, on a SMALL box (it may stick
  out past the box, that is intended), and at the very top of the page (it tucks inside instead of
  going off-screen). Try Circle and Point too. Then change the annotation colour in the model's
  settings, including a pale one like yellow: the text must stay readable on every colour.

- NEW: THE NUMBER MUST VARY. Confidences used to all read 1.00. Now they should differ per box,
  and if you raise Detection Confidence in the model's settings to 50%, every label you can see
  must read 0.50 or higher. Findings saved before this build still show their old 100% — expected,
  the real value was never stored.

- NEW: ONE QUESTION AT A TIME. Both the telemetry question and the back-up question are now the
  same style of card. To get both at once: save 2-3 findings signed out, then Settings > Restore
  Defaults, then sign in. Telemetry must come FIRST, alone; the back-up question must appear only
  after you answer it, and it must NOT be forgotten. Tap fast on "Not now" — the second tap must
  NOT hit the back-up card's buttons underneath. Check both cards on iPad and with Larger Text
  turned up: they should stay a centred card and never fill the whole window.

- NEW: A FAILED CONVERSION MUST BE REPORTABLE. Import a model the converter cannot handle. The error
  must offer "Report Failing Conversion", and the draft must name the model's own filename, not just
  a phase (the log shows it too, under Model file). An error after a conversion SUCCEEDED must NOT
  offer it. With no mail set up it must open the log, which still shares, not do nothing.

- NEW: YOU WILL BE SIGNED OUT ONCE. This build uses the real account system, not the test one, so
  sign in again. Findings you BACKED UP earlier do not carry over; the ones on your device are
  untouched. Synchronize must then FINISH, on a Mac too, where it has never once worked.

- YOUR EXISTING FINDINGS MUST ALL STILL BE THERE. Storage changed. Update from the build you were
  running rather than deleting the app first — a fresh install cannot test this. Every finding
  still listed, with its picture, title and box in the right place.

- TWO DEVICES, ONE ACCOUNT. On A, open Findings and leave it open. On B, edit a finding's title.
  On A, close Findings and reopen: B's change should be there without you pressing anything.

- IF YOU HAVE EVER TAPPED "Keep on This Device": Synchronize must leave those findings local,
  however many times you press it. Only "Back Up My Findings", inside the question-mark sheet,
  still sends them.

- A shared link points where the detection actually was. Before saving, pinch and drag to crop in
  tight. Share with the link on and open it: the mark under "Where on the page" should sit where
  the thing was. Findings saved before this build show no such section: deliberate.

- Deleting your account offers your findings first: Delete Account must say they are NOT deleted
  with the account and offer "Export First…". With nothing saved, that choice must NOT appear.

- Subscribing on an Apple Account that already holds the subscription must let you in or point you
  at Restore Purchases, never dead-end.
