Test Focus 1: Findings can now be shared as a link that opens the app on that finding.

Sharing a finding still shares the picture. What is new is that a link travels with it, and the card itself now carries a QR code and the address link.aiarcheology.com. Anyone who sees your post can get to the finding; anyone without the app gets a page describing it and a link to the App Store.

Please put coordinates into at least one finding before you start (Findings list, tap a finding, GPS field, e.g. 48.8, 19.53). Without them the map part cannot be tested at all.

1. Share a finding. The card is noticeably larger than before, and the QR sits in the bottom-right of the information block, under the date. Check Portrait and Landscape, Light and Dark: the code must never overlap the text.

2. The new switch "Let people open this finding in the app" is on. Send the finding to yourself in Messages or Mail. Both the picture AND a link should arrive.

3. Turn that switch off and share again. "Save Image" comes back in the share sheet, and no link is attached. That is the trade-off the switch exists for — with a link attached, iOS hides Save Image and any app that only accepts pictures.

4. Tap the link you sent yourself. The app should open on a read-only screen saying "This Is Your Finding" — because it is yours. Nothing new should appear in your Findings list.

5. Send a link to someone else with the app installed. They should see the finding with an orange "Shared by Someone Else" warning, the label, confidence, your name and the date. It must NOT be added to their findings.

6. On that screen, if the finding had coordinates, there is a "Show on Map" button. The map should appear only after tapping it, never before.

7. Tap "Open the Original Page". The app should switch to the browser and load the page, with the drawer closed.

8. Open a link on a device WITHOUT the app, or on a computer. You should get a page describing the finding with a "Get ML Browser" button.

9. Damage a link on purpose — delete a chunk from the end and open it. The app must say "This Link Could Not Be Read", never open to nothing.

10. Point the Camera app at the QR on a shared card, including a photo of the card on another screen. It should scan.

Test Focus 2: Findings carry the name of whoever found them.

The Save screen has an Author field between Note and GPS, prefilled from your account when signed in and empty otherwise. Save one with a name and one with it cleared — a blank author must show no row at all on the card, not "Unknown". Signed out it must never show your email address. Export (Findings list, Export) should include an author column.

Most important: please confirm every finding you had BEFORE this build is still there, with its picture, title, note and GPS intact. If anything is missing, or the app will not start, tell us immediately and say which version you updated from.

Please report your device model, iOS version and the language you tested in.
