Build: Findings you were sent

Test Focus: A finding someone shares with you is now kept. The findings list has two tabs — My and
Received — and a finding that arrived from a link is read-only, so it stays exactly as its sender
wrote it.

- Ask someone to share a finding with you, or share one from a second device. Tap the link. The
  finding opens, and it is saved without you being asked. Tap Done, open the findings list, and
  switch to Received: it is there. Switch to My: it must NOT be there.

- Tap the same link a second time. You should still have exactly one copy of that finding, not two.

- Tap a link while the app is completely closed (swipe it away from the app switcher first). The
  finding must still appear — this used to be swallowed silently.

- Open a received finding from the Received tab. Its title, note, author, coordinate and page
  cannot be edited and there is no Save button, but Share still works. Where a photo would be, you
  see an outline showing where the detection sat — a link carries no picture, so that is expected
  for now.

- Check the date on a received finding. It should be the day the sender captured it, with no time
  of day, and it should not shift by a day. Share that finding onward and confirm the date it
  carries matches what you were shown.

- On an iPhone in landscape, tap a link. The Done button must be reachable — the card must not be
  cut off at the top.

- Your own findings are unaffected: capture one, confirm it appears under My, and that you can
  still edit and save it.

- THE ONE-COPY RULE, THE HARD WAY. The test above uses a short link; this one uses the worst case.
  Capture a finding on a page with a very long web address (a map portal with a lot of parameters
  is ideal), give it a long title and an author, and share it as an IMAGE — the card with the QR
  code on it. Scan that code with a second device, then scan it AGAIN. You must end up with one
  copy in Received, not two. Before this build a long address could push the finding's identity off
  the code, and every scan of the same printed card made another copy.

- Scan the same card from further away and at an angle, and in poor light. It must still scan — the
  code was NOT made denser to fit more in.

- A link you were sent by an earlier TestFlight build must still open and still be recognised as
  the same finding, not arrive as a new copy.
