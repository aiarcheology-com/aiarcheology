Test Focus: More ONNX models convert on your device

What's new:

• The on-device ONNX converter now handles more image-classification models: ResNet-50 and SqueezeNet from the ONNX Model Zoo convert and run, alongside the previously supported MobileNetV2.

Please test:

1. Open the model importer and choose Download from URL.
2. Paste https://github.com/onnx/models/raw/main/validated/vision/classification/squeezenet/model/squeezenet1.0-12.onnx and import — conversion should finish without errors.
3. Enable the converted model, browse to any page with photos, and confirm classification labels appear.
4. Repeat with ResNet-50 if you have time (larger download, ~100 MB): https://github.com/onnx/models/raw/main/validated/vision/classification/resnet/model/resnet50-v1-7.onnx
5. Re-import a MobileNetV2 ONNX model if you have one — it should still convert and classify as before.

Known limitations:

• Object-detection ONNX models (e.g. YOLO) are not convertible yet and are rejected with a clear message.
