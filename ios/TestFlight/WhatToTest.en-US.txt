Build: Aim a finding with the centre crosshair

Test Focus: Saving a finding used to be a single tap anywhere on the page. It is now an aiming
step. Tap Find (the crosshair button in the top-right) and a large copy of that same icon — four
times as wide and four times as tall as the small one in the hint bar — appears in the middle of
the web content. Tapping it always saves a finding; its colour says what kind. RED means nothing
was detected under it and the place itself is stored. GREEN means a detected rectangle covers it
and that rectangle is stored.

- With no model running, tap Find and then tap the red crosshair. The finding form must open for
  the spot under it. Save it and check the finding in the list. This is the "record something the
  models did not find" case and it must work with detection switched off entirely.

- Only a small area in the very middle of the crosshair reacts to a tap. Everywhere else on the
  page — including most of the drawn crosshair — must still pan, pinch-zoom and flick normally.
  If the page will not move while the crosshair is up, that is the most important bug to report.

- Now enable a model and start detection on a page where rectangles are found. Pan the page to
  bring one under the crosshair; it must turn green. Tap it and confirm the form opens for that
  rectangle, with its image and label, rather than for a blank spot.

- Detection keeps running the whole time you are aiming: the coloured rectangles should refresh and
  follow the content after each pan or pinch settles, rather than staying stuck where they were.

- Move the page again in the moment between the tap and the form appearing. The form must not open
  on the old position; you should be left aiming, with a red crosshair, until the page settles.

- Cancel in the hint bar must leave aiming mode at any point without saving anything, and detection
  must carry on as before.

- The guided tour is deliberately unchanged: its map lesson is still the old single tap, with no
  large crosshair. Run the tour from Settings and confirm it still completes.
