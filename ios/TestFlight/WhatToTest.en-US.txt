Test Focus 1: Findings can now be shared as a link that opens the app on that finding.

Sharing a finding still shares the picture. What is new is that a link travels with it, and the card itself now carries a QR code and the address link.aiarcheology.com. Anyone who sees your post can get to the finding; anyone without the app gets a page describing it and a link to the App Store.

Please put coordinates into at least one finding before you start (Findings list, tap a finding, GPS field, e.g. 48.8, 19.53). Without them the map part cannot be tested at all.

1. Share a finding. The card is noticeably larger than before, and the QR sits flush in the bottom-right CORNER of the information block, beside the rows rather than under them. Check Portrait and Landscape, Light and Dark, with a finding that has many rows (label, note, GPS, host, date) and one with only two: the code must be in the corner every time and must never overlap the text. Landscape cards are wider than in the previous build; that is deliberate.

2. The new switch "Let people open this finding in the app" is on. Send the finding to yourself in Messages or Mail. Both the picture AND a link should arrive.

3. Turn that switch off and share again. "Save Image" comes back in the share sheet, and no link is attached. That is the trade-off the switch exists for — with a link attached, iOS hides Save Image and any app that only accepts pictures.

4. Tap the link you sent yourself. The app should open on a read-only screen saying "This Is Your Finding" — because it is yours. Nothing new should appear in your Findings list.

5. Send a link to someone else with the app installed. They should see the finding with an orange "Shared by Someone Else" warning, the label, confidence, your name and the date. It must NOT be added to their findings.

6. On that screen, if the finding had coordinates, there is a "Show on Map" button. The map should appear only after tapping it, never before.

7. Tap "Open the Original Page". The app should switch to the browser and load the page, with the drawer closed. On a fresh install, do this while the welcome screen or the tour is still pending: it must land on the shared page, not on the app's demo page.

8. Open a link on a device WITHOUT the app, or on a computer. You should get a page describing the finding with a "Get ML Browser" button.

9. Damage a link on purpose — delete a chunk from the end and open it. The app must say "This Link Could Not Be Read", never open to nothing.

10. Point the Camera app at the QR on a shared card, including a photo of the card on another screen. It should scan. The code is now sized to what it holds, so expect a slightly larger one for a long map address and the usual size for a short one.

Test Focus 2: A shared link now says WHERE the finding is, not just what it is.

Two things changed in what travels. A shared link now carries the FULL web address of the page, including the part after the "?" that map sites use to remember where you were looking — so a recipient lands on your view, not the site's front page. And the composer shows you that exact address before you post anything, with a switch to reduce it to the site alone.

Please test with a real map portal (ags.cuzk.cz, mapy.cz, openstreetmap.org). That is where this matters.

1. Find something on a map portal, save it, share it. Under the link switch there is a box headed "This link carries at most:" showing the web address. Whatever is in that box is what will travel — nothing more. Read it.

2. Turn "Share the full web address" off: the address shortens to the site alone. Turn it back on: the long form returns.

3. Send that link to yourself and open it elsewhere. It must land on the SAME map view you shared, not the portal's home page. This is the point of the change; please report it if you land somewhere else.

4. Share the same finding with the switch off. Now it should land on the site's front page. Both are correct — the switch chooses.

5. Open a shared link. The finding now appears as a CARD over the dimmed app rather than a full screen. On a Mac it should be a card in the middle of the window, not a page with a few lines stranded in the corner.

6. New on that card: "Where on the page" — a small diagram of a page with a red box showing where the detection sat. It is a diagram, not a screenshot: no picture of your page ever leaves your device.

7. The important one. Sign out, or use a fresh install, so the paywall, the welcome screen or the analytics question is still waiting — then open a shared link. The finding must appear ON TOP of that screen. In the previous build it waited behind it and you saw a wall instead.

8. From that card, still behind the paywall, tap "Open the Original Page". The card closes, you deal with the paywall as usual, and once through, the app should land on the shared page.

9. Tap Done instead: the card closes and does not come back. Tapping outside the card must NOT close it.

10. Open a shared link while Settings or another sheet is open. It should appear when you close that sheet, not flash and vanish.

Test Focus 3: Findings carry the name of whoever found them.

The Save screen has an Author field between Note and GPS, prefilled from your account when signed in and empty otherwise. Save one with a name and one with it cleared — a blank author must show no row at all on the card, not "Unknown". Signed out it must never show your email address. Export (Findings list, Export) should include an author column.

Most important: please confirm every finding you had BEFORE this build is still there, with its picture, title, note and GPS intact. If anything is missing, or the app will not start, tell us immediately and say which version you updated from.

Please report your device model, iOS version and the language you tested in.
