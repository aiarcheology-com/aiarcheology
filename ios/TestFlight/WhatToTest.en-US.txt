Test Focus 1: Sharing a finding as a link — and what that link now carries.

Sharing still shares the picture; a link travels beside it and the card carries a QR. What changed: the link now holds the FULL web address, including the part after the "?" that map sites keep the view in — so a recipient lands on YOUR view, not the site's front page. You are shown that exact address before you post.

Please test on a real map portal (ags.cuzk.cz, mapy.com, openstreetmap.org). Put coordinates into one finding first (Findings, tap one, GPS field, e.g. 48.8, 19.53) or the map part cannot be tested.

1. Share a finding from a map portal. Under the link switch is a box headed "This link carries at most:" showing the web address. Read it — whatever is in that box is what travels, nothing more.

2. Turn "Share the full web address" off: it shortens to the site alone. Turn it back on: the long form returns.

3. Send that link to yourself and open it on another device. It must land on the SAME map view, not the portal's home page. Report it if you land elsewhere.

4. Share again with the switch off: it lands on the site's front page. Both are correct; the switch chooses.

5. The QR sits flush in the bottom-right CORNER of the information block, beside the rows rather than under them. Check Portrait and Landscape, Light and Dark, with a finding that has many rows (label, note, GPS, host, date) and one with two. It must never overlap text. Landscape cards are wider than before; that is deliberate.

6. Scan the QR with the Code Scanner (Control Centre), including from a photo of the card on another screen: it opens the app on the finding. The Camera app opens the web page instead — that is iOS, not a fault. The code is sized to what it holds, so expect a bigger one for a long address.

7. Turn "Let people open this finding in the app" off and share: "Save Image" comes back and no link is attached. That is the trade-off the switch exists for.

8. Open a link to your OWN finding: the app opens on a read-only screen saying it is yours. Nothing is added to your Findings list — a shared link never creates a finding.

9. Open someone else's link. It appears as a CARD over the dimmed app, not a full screen — on a Mac a card in the middle of the window, not lines stranded in a corner. It carries an orange "Shared by Someone Else" warning, and a "Where on the page" diagram of where the detection sat. A diagram, not a screenshot: no picture of your page ever leaves your device.

10. The important one. Sign out or use a fresh install so the paywall, welcome screen or analytics question is still waiting, then open a shared link. The finding must appear ON TOP of it. Then tap "Open the Original Page" from there: the card closes, you deal with the paywall, and you should land on the shared page.

11. Dismiss (top left) closes the card and it does not come back. Tapping outside the card must NOT close it.

12. Damage a link on purpose (delete a chunk from the end) and open it. The app must say it could not read the link, never open to nothing.

13. Open a link on a device WITHOUT the app, or on a computer: a page describing the finding with a "Get ML Browser" button, whose page link opens the same map view.

Test Focus 2: Findings carry the name of whoever found them.

The Save screen has an Author field between Note and GPS, prefilled from your account when signed in and empty otherwise. Save one with a name and one with it cleared — a blank author must show no row at all on the card, not "Unknown". Signed out it must never show your email address. Export (Findings list, Export) should include an author column.

Most important: please confirm every finding you had BEFORE this build is still there, with its picture, title, note and GPS intact. If anything is missing, or the app will not start, tell us immediately and say which version you updated from.

Please report your device model, iOS version and the language you tested in.
