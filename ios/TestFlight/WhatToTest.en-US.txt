Test Focus: The app can now show messages from us — as a card at the top of the screen (news, questions with buttons) and in Settings → About App. Messages arrive remotely; no update is needed for us to publish or withdraw one.

Please test (a test message must be live — check with the team which ones are running):

1. Launch the app. If a message campaign is live, a card appears at the top of the screen within a few seconds — over the sign-in screen, the subscription screen, or the app itself, depending on the campaign.
2. Read the card: title and text should be fully visible and readable, in light and dark mode, also after rotating the device.
3. If the card has no buttons: tap the ✕ to close it. It should slide away and NOT come back — not even after force-quitting and relaunching the app.
4. If the card has answer buttons (e.g. Yes / No): tap one. The card should disappear and not return. If another message is waiting, it appears next — one card at a time.
5. Open Settings → ML Browser → About App: campaign messages aimed at this screen are listed at the top. They stay visible there for their whole runtime (closing the top card does not remove them here).
6. Signed out? The sign-in screen only ever shows plain announcements — never cards with answer buttons.
7. If a campaign uses notifications: with the app closed, a quiet notification may appear in Notification Center (no sound or banner — that is intended). Tapping it opens the app directly on that message.
