Build: TBD

Test Focus: the app now speaks 16 languages properly. Until now it claimed to support them but only about a quarter of the text was translated — set your phone to Czech and most of the app still came out in English. Every screen is now translated, including the subscription and account screens, which were the last ones left in English.

Two things before you start. The translations were produced by machine, not a professional translator, so awkward or wrong wording is a genuinely useful report. And the wording on the subscription screens is the part we most want checked, because getting billing text wrong has consequences beyond looking sloppy.

To test, change your phone's language in Settings > General > Language & Region, then reopen the app.

1. Pick any of Arabic, Bengali, Czech, German, Spanish, Persian, French, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian or the three Chinese variants. Walk through the side menu, Settings, the model catalog, downloads, importing a model, and the findings list. Everything should be in your language. Report any screen still mostly English.

2. Open the subscribe screen and read the money copy end to end. The price, what a subscription includes, the free-trial line, and the small legal paragraph should all be in your language and should agree with each other. This paragraph must still say when payment is taken, that the subscription renews automatically, that you can cancel up to 24 hours before the period ends, and where to cancel. If any of that is missing or contradicts the rest of the screen, that is the most important thing to report in this build.

3. Settings > Subscription, and the account-deletion confirmation, should also be fully translated. The deletion warning must make it unmistakable that the action cannot be undone.

4. Set the language to Arabic, Hebrew or Persian. These read right to left, and this is the first build with enough real text to see it properly. The layout should mirror — menus, arrows and back buttons on the opposite side. Look for text running off the edge, overlapping, or reading in the wrong direction. This is the likeliest place for something to look broken.

5. Set the language to German or Russian. Both run noticeably longer than English. Check buttons, Settings rows and tab labels for text cut off with "…" or spilling outside its button. A screenshot helps.

6. Find a screen that counts things, such as a findings or file count. Check it reads naturally for 0, 1, 2 and 5. Czech, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian and Arabic change the word depending on the number, and this is the first build to get that right.

7. Anywhere a number, price or name is inserted into a sentence ("Files: 3 / 10", "$3.99 / month", "The model X from Y has been imported"), confirm the values still appear and sit sensibly in the sentence. Missing or duplicated values there are a real bug, not a wording preference.

8. On first launch, iOS asks permission to find devices on your local network. That prompt is now translated too — check it reads naturally in your language.

9. Switch back to English and confirm nothing changed: English text should be exactly as it was before this build.
