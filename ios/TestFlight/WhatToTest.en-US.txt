Test Focus: The order of the opening screens has changed. You now reach the subscribe screen first, and signing in is optional — offered when it is actually useful rather than demanded up front. Nothing about what the app *does* has changed.

Background: previously the app asked you to sign in before it would show you anything, including the price. That is a lot to ask of someone who has not seen the app work yet, and it meant a paying subscriber with no internet connection was locked out of features that run entirely on their own device. Access is now decided by your subscription; your account is for saving findings and syncing them later.

This build also contains changes to our own build and testing setup. They do not touch the app, so there is nothing extra to try because of them.

Please test:

1. Fresh install, no subscription: you should reach the subscribe screen without being asked to sign in. Confirm the screen offers Sign In near the bottom, alongside Subscribe and Restore.
2. If you subscribe (or already have), you should go straight into the app without ever signing in. Use it normally for a while.
3. Turn on Airplane Mode and launch the app while subscribed. You should get straight in. Previously this showed a "needs connection" screen — if you see one, that is the most important bug in this build.
4. Sign in from each of the four places, signing out again between each one — otherwise the other entry points disappear once you are signed in. The four are: the subscribe screen, the side menu (bottom), Settings, and the offer that appears once after you save a finding.
5. Save a finding while signed out. The finding must be saved whether or not you sign in afterwards — choosing "Cancel" on the sign-in offer must never lose it. Save several more findings and confirm the offer does not appear again: it is meant to be shown once per install.
6. While signed in, open Settings and sign out. You should stay exactly where you are, still in the app. Previously this threw you out to a sign-in screen. If you are asked about analytics at any point after signing in or out, it should appear as a panel over the app — the page you were on must still be there behind it, on the same scroll position.
7. If you are not subscribed and you sign out from the subscribe screen, confirm the Sign Out button is replaced by Sign In, and that you can sign back in and reach Delete Account.
8. Analytics consent: if you are asked whether to enable analytics, that question should come after the subscribe screen, and you should be asked even if you never sign in. Answer it, then check Settings shows the same answer you gave.
9. Report immediately if you are ever asked to sign in before you can see the price, or if a subscribed user is blocked from the app for any reason. Those are the two failures this change could plausibly cause, and they matter more than anything else in this build.

Also in this build: going offline no longer wipes the page you have open.

10. Load any page, then turn on Airplane Mode. The page must stay exactly as it was — readable, scrollable, and detection and classification still work on it.
11. While offline, tap a link, reload, or enter an address: nothing loads, and no error screen replaces your page. The silence is deliberate — keeping your page beats an error screen. The address bar may show the address that failed. (Some sites, like some news portals, serve their own "you are offline" page from cache; that message comes from the site, not from the app.)
12. "Page didn't load / Try Again" should now appear only when there was nothing on screen worth keeping — for example relaunching the app while offline. From there, Try Again must load the page normally once you are back online.

Also in this build: a new install gets 14 days of free access, and the opening screens have been split in two.

A fresh install now hands you 14 free days, and the first thing you see is a **Welcome screen**: the app logo, "Explore everything free for 14 days", and a single Continue button. It asks for nothing — no price to accept, no card, no subscribe button. Tapping Continue starts the guided tour, and the analytics question comes after that.

The screen that asks for money is now a **separate** screen, and you only meet it once your 14 days are up (or if you open Settings > Subscription yourself). It states what a subscription includes, and from day 14 there is no way past it.

The mid-period reminder that used to reappear on day 7 has been removed. Not seeing it is correct.

You do not have to wait two weeks to test this. Change the device date in iOS Settings to jump forward — General > Date & Time, turn off "Set Automatically". Moving the clock **forward** works normally. Moving it **backwards** deliberately will not give you more free time back; that is intended, not a bug.

13. Fresh install: the Welcome screen must be the **first** thing you see — before the guided tour, not after it. Confirm it has a Continue button and no Subscribe button, and that no tour bubbles are visible behind it. Tap Continue: the tour should start, and the analytics question should follow only once the tour is finished or skipped. The order is Welcome, then tour, then analytics.
14. Relaunch the app a few times over the next days. On days 1 to 13 you should see no Welcome screen and no subscribe screen at all. Either of them appearing on every launch is the main thing to report.
15. Move the date forward past day 14 and relaunch. You should now meet the subscribe screen, with no way past it. It should say your free access has ended. Check that "Export or Delete Findings" is offered there if you saved anything, and that exporting actually produces a file — being unable to reach your own findings at this point is a serious bug.
16. While inside the free period, open Settings and tap Subscription. It should open the subscribe screen — and it must NOT say your free access has ended, because it has not. It should simply offer the subscription.

17. Delete the app and reinstall it while your free period is running. **It must pick up where it left off, not start a fresh 14 days.** If reinstalling resets the counter, report it — that is the most important thing in this section.
18. If you subscribe at any point, the free-period messaging should disappear entirely and never wall you, even after day 14 has passed.
19. On the subscribe screen, read the money copy as a whole and check it does not contradict itself. If you are being offered a free trial, the button says "Start ... Free Trial", the line under it says nothing is charged during the trial, and the legal paragraph at the bottom should agree — it must say payment is charged when the trial ends, not "at confirmation". If you have already used the free trial on this Apple Account, it should say plainly that billing starts as soon as you subscribe, instead of promising a trial you would not get.
20. On an iPad, open a second window of the app (drag the icon to the side of the screen) during your first launch, while the Welcome screen is up. The tour must not start in either window while the Welcome screen is showing anywhere. This is fiddly to reach and worth trying once.

Also in this build: the subscribe screen now says what the price recurs over.

The price used to read as a bare "$3.99", which never said per what, and the screen never said what a subscription actually gets you. Both now come from the App Store listing itself rather than being typed into the app, so they cannot drift from what you are really charged.

21. Open the subscribe screen and read the price. It must say "$3.99 / month", not "$3.99". Above it you should see a short line describing the subscription ("Full access to ML Browser."), and below it a short list of what is included.
22. Check the same on the wall you reach after the free period ends. That is the screen someone actually buys from, and a price with no period there is the one thing this change exists to prevent.
23. Turn VoiceOver on and swipe to the price. It should be spoken as a price per month, not with the word "slash" in it.
24. Set Settings > Accessibility > Display & Text Size > Larger Text to the largest size and reopen the subscribe screen. Two lines got longer, so this is where they would break: confirm nothing is cut off mid-word, and scroll if needed to confirm Subscribe, Restore, Continue (if present) and the Terms/Privacy links are all tappable.
25. Open Settings > Subscription while subscribed and confirm the plan row still reads "$3.99 / month". It was already correct there; both screens now share one piece of code, so a break in one would show up in the other.
26. Rotate an iPhone and iPad to landscape on **both** new screens and scroll each from top to bottom. On the Welcome screen: logo, headline, the note about what happens afterwards, the three reassurance lines, and Continue. On the subscribe screen: logo, price, what is included, the purchase button, Restore, account controls and the Terms/Privacy links. Nothing may be clipped off-screen on either.

Nothing else in this build is visible to you: a screen left over from the old sign-in-first flow was deleted (it had been unreachable since the subscribe screen became the first thing you see), and an internal setup document was brought up to date.

Note for testers: the Subscribe button carries an "under construction" badge in TestFlight builds. That is expected — purchases here run against Apple's Sandbox and never charge real money.

Also in this build: the app now speaks 16 languages properly.

Until now the app claimed to support Arabic, Bengali, Czech, German, Spanish, Persian, French, Hebrew, Hindi, Hungarian, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian and three kinds of Chinese — but only about a quarter of the text was actually translated. If you set your phone to any of those languages, most of the app still came out in English. Roughly four thousand missing pieces of text have now been translated, so those languages should read as complete.

Two things to know before you start. First, the translations were produced by machine, not by a professional translator — they are marked internally as needing review, and finding awkward or wrong wording is genuinely useful, so please report it. Second, the subscription, payment and account-deletion screens are deliberately still in English. That is not an oversight: getting billing or account-deletion wording wrong in a language we cannot read is a real risk, so those wait for a human translator.

To test, change your phone's language in iOS Settings > General > Language & Region, then reopen the app.

27. Pick any of the listed languages and walk through the app — the side menu, Settings, the model catalog, downloads, importing a model, and the findings list. The text should be in your language throughout. Report any screen that is still mostly English (other than the subscription and account-deletion screens, which are expected to be English for now).
28. Set the language to Arabic, Hebrew or Persian. These read right-to-left, and this is the first build where they contain enough real text to see properly. The whole layout should mirror: menus, arrows and back buttons should sit on the opposite side. Look for text running off the edge, overlapping, or reading in the wrong direction — this is the single most likely place for something to look broken.
29. Set the language to German or Russian. Both are noticeably longer than English. Check buttons, Settings rows and the tab labels for text that is cut off with "…" or spills outside its button. Report anything unreadable, with a screenshot.
30. Find a screen that counts things — for example a findings or file count. Check it reads naturally for different numbers, especially 1, 2, 5 and 0. Languages like Czech, Polish, Russian, Ukrainian and Arabic change the word depending on the number, and this build is the first to get that right, so a wrong-sounding count is worth reporting.
31. Anywhere a number or name is inserted into a sentence ("Files: 3 / 10", "The model X from Y has been imported"), confirm the numbers and names still appear and are in a sensible place in the sentence. Missing or duplicated numbers here would be a real bug, not a wording preference.
32. Switch your phone back to English and confirm nothing changed: English text should be exactly as it was before this build.
