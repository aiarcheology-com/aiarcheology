Build: AIA-0817

Test Focus: a finding saved on a map site must record WHERE it was — the exact page address, and a
coordinate when the map will tell us one. Both fields are yours to edit, and yours to withhold when
you share.

- NEW: THE PAGE ADDRESS IS A FIELD YOU CAN EDIT. Save a finding anywhere; the Page row is no longer
  grey text. Select, copy, correct or paste a URL. Do the same on a saved finding, leave the screen
  and come back: your change must have stuck. Before this build a wrong address was permanent.

- NEW: GPS FILLS ITSELF IN ON MAP SITES. Open a map site that shows coordinates in its address bar —
  freemap.sk, caltopo.com, en.mapy.cz, zbgis.skgeodesy.sk all do. Pan and zoom to somewhere
  recognisable, then save a finding. The GPS field should already contain something like
  "url: 47.941230, 18.709545" or "leaflet: 47.94123, 18.709545". Check the numbers against what the
  map is showing: they must match the place you are looking at, not somewhere else.

- NEW: THE PREFIX TELLS YOU WHERE THE NUMBER CAME FROM. "leaflet:", "openlayers:", "arcgis:" and
  "maplibre:" mean the map itself was asked. "url:" means it was read from the address. "map:" means
  a map we could not name. Whatever it says, you can edit or clear it — and it must NOT come back or
  change on its own while the save sheet is open. Both fields sit right under the title, Page then
  GPS, each with its own copy button.

- NEW: YOU CAN SHARE A FINDING WITHOUT SAYING WHERE IT IS. Share a finding that has a coordinate:
  the composer now offers "Share where this finding is". Turn it off and the coordinate must vanish
  from the card AND from the link — open the shared link and check there is no map pin. Turn it on
  and both come back. On a finding with no coordinate the switch must not appear at all.

- NEW: MAPS THAT HIDE THEIR ADDRESS SHOULD STILL GIVE A POSITION. Try ags.cuzk.gov.cz/av/ — its
  address never changes as you pan, which is the case this build is for. Save a finding: ideally GPS
  holds an "arcgis:" value; if empty, that is a known limit, but Page must still hold the address.

- WORTH BREAKING: pan a map, then IMMEDIATELY save a finding. The recorded address must be where you
  ended up, not where you started. Also type a new address in the bar and, while it is still
  loading, tap a detection on the page still showing — the finding must record the page you could
  SEE, not the one being loaded. Tap twice in quick succession while the app is working out the
  location: exactly one save sheet must appear.

- WORTH BREAKING: put nonsense in the Page field ("not a url") and save. The finding must still
  save, keeping the address it had. Clear the field completely and save: that must be respected —
  the finding then has no page. Neither must ever lose the finding or its picture.

- REGRESSION: save findings on ordinary, non-map pages too — a news article, a photo. GPS must stay
  EMPTY there; an invented coordinate is far worse than none. (A page that publishes its own
  location tag, as some venue and event pages do, may legitimately fill it in — not a bug.)
  Share a finding with a long note in BOTH layouts: landscape must show far more of it than before
  and must not cut the coordinate, the site or the date off the bottom. Check a long coordinate is
  not truncated. Export findings to CSV and confirm the gps column reads sensibly.

- REGRESSION (AIA-0822): after a local reset, detection must work on the first try. If the app opens
  with an empty model list, turn on a bundled demo model and start detection. Boxes must appear in
  that same session, with no restart and no duplicate boxes.

- KNOWN: some map sites will give only the address and no coordinate. That is expected for now.
  Google Maps and a few heavily-bundled sites are known gaps.
