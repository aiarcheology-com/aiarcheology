Test Focus 1: Sharing a finding as a link, and what it carries.

A link now travels beside the shared picture, and the card carries a QR. It holds the FULL address, including the part after the "?" holding a map's view, so a recipient lands on YOUR view. Use a real map portal (ags.cuzk.cz, mapy.com, openstreetmap.org) and put coordinates in one finding first (Findings, tap one, GPS, e.g. 48.8, 19.53), or the map part cannot be tested.

1. Share a finding from a map portal. The box headed "This link carries at most:" shows exactly what travels. NEW: the copy button beside it puts that address on the clipboard - paste and compare.

2. Turn "Share the full web address" off: it shortens to the site alone, and copying gives the shorter one. Turn it back on: the long form returns.

3. Send that link to yourself and open it on another device: it must land on the SAME map view. With the switch off it lands on the site's front page - both are correct, the switch chooses.

4. The QR sits flush in the bottom-right CORNER of the information block, beside the rows not under them. Check Portrait and Landscape, Light and Dark, with a finding of many rows and one of two; it must not overlap text.

5. Scan the QR with the Code Scanner (Control Centre), including from a photo of the card on another screen: it opens the app on the finding. The Camera app opens the web page - that is iOS, not a bug.

6. Turn "Let people open this finding in the app" off and share: "Save Image" returns, no link attached.

7. Open a link to your OWN finding: it opens read-only, saying it is yours. Nothing is added to your Findings list.

8. Open someone else's link. It appears as a CARD over the dimmed app, not full screen, with an orange "Shared by Someone Else" warning and a "Where on the page" diagram - no picture of your page leaves your device.

9. NEW, look hardest at this. On that card "Open the Original Page" is the big button under the title, with the FULL address printed beneath it, wrapping, plus a copy button. Read it before you tap: a link can no longer show a familiar site name while sending you elsewhere.

10. Sign out or use a fresh install so the paywall, welcome screen or analytics question is still waiting, then open a shared link. The finding must appear ON TOP of it. Then "Open the Original Page" closes the card and lands on the shared page.

11. Dismiss (top left) closes the card for good. Tapping outside must NOT close it.

12. Damage a link (delete a chunk from the end): the app must say it could not read it, never open to nothing.

13. Open a link on a device WITHOUT the app, or on a computer: a page describing the finding with a "Get ML Browser" button.

Test Focus 2: Findings carry the name of whoever found them.

The Save screen has an Author field between Note and GPS, prefilled from your account when signed in and empty otherwise. Save one with a name and one cleared - a blank author must show no row on the card, not "Unknown". Signed out it must never show your email. Export should include an author column.

Test Focus 3: Deleting a finding on a Mac.

On a Mac, right-click a finding row: Delete now sits beside Share, in red. It removes the finding and its picture just as swiping does on iPhone and iPad. Delete one, then the last one, and confirm the empty screen. Touch is unchanged: swipe left, no menu on long press.

Test Focus 4: The download screen.

The hint now reads ".mlmodel or .onnx", not ".mlmodel" alone - check it in each language you read, endings still in Latin letters. Downloading by address, Open Catalog and Files imports must behave as before: a model that imported before but is refused now matters most.

This build also carries groundwork for converting larger detection models. It changes nothing you can see, so there is nothing new to try for it - but if anything above behaves differently than it did, say so, because it should not.

Most important: confirm every finding you had BEFORE this build is still there, with picture, title, note and GPS intact. If anything is missing, or the app will not start, tell us at once and say which version you came from.

Please report your device model, iOS version and the language you tested in.
